#Volatility Smile Analysis

Empirical analysis of implied volatility patterns in DAX options and warrants traded on EUREX and EUWAX.

Research focus

Differences between EUREX options and EUWAX warrants
Impact of moneyness and maturity on implied volatility
Relationship between commodity prices and volatility

Methods

Black-Scholes implied volatility
Quadratic moneyness regression
Interaction effects
Statistical hypothesis testing

Data

DAX derivatives
EUREX & EUWAX
Natural Gas, Crude Oil, Gold & Silver
30 May 2022 – 15 March 2023

Key findings

Significant differences in volatility smiles between EUREX and EUWAX, particularly for short maturities.
Call options exhibit U-shaped volatility smiles, while puts tend to show volatility smirks.
Volatility structures flatten as maturity increases.
Commodity prices show significant relationships with implied volatility.
